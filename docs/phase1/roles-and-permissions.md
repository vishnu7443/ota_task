# Role and Permission Analysis

**Source Basis:** Official Problem Statement Section 3 (5 Users & Roles), Section 6 (Minimum 3 Roles in Must-Have; Owner in Should-Have; RBAC explicitly mandatory per Section 3: *"Role-based access is part of the requirement, not an extra"*).  
**Architectural Design Decision:** Implement full 5-role RBAC cleanly from Day 1 so there is zero permission bleed between operational data-entry roles, audit oversight, and executive reporting.

---

## 1. Role Profiles & Device Ergonomics

### 1.1 Salesman (`SALESMAN`)
- **Primary Device:** Smartphone (portrait, single-thumb operation, unreliable 3G/4G field connectivity).
- **Design Goal (Section 9):** Place an order in **under 1 minute**.
- **Scope Boundary:** Sees **only** customers assigned to them (or active retail/distributor catalog if territories aren't exclusive — see `AMB-09`), **only** their own submitted orders, and **only** customer-wise sales generated by their own orders (or assigned customers).
- **Allowed Actions:**
  - Authenticate & manage own session.
  - Browse/search assigned active customers.
  - View finished product catalog & active selling prices (with non-blocking stock indicator badge if permitted, never raw-material/cost data).
  - Create, save draft locally, and submit Sales Orders.
  - Edit orders **only** while in `DRAFT` or `CORRECTION_REQUESTED` status.
  - Track live status of own orders (`SUBMITTED`, `APPROVED`, `INVOICED`, `DISPATCHED`, `REJECTED`).
  - View own customer-wise sales summary & download/share customer invoice PDF once Admin raises it (optional convenience).
- **Explicitly Forbidden Actions:**
  - Cannot approve, reject, invoice, or dispatch orders (no self-approval).
  - Cannot view or edit another salesman's orders or commissions/sales totals.
  - Cannot override standard price list prices unless explicitly enabled by Admin policy (default: read-only price).
  - Cannot view raw-material stock, BOM recipes, production runs, production costs/margins, or supplier purchase rates.
  - Cannot record payments or issue credit notes.
  - Cannot alter master data (customers, products, GST rates, price lists) or view Audit Logs.

### 1.2 Factory Admin (`FACTORY_ADMIN`)
- **Primary Device:** Desktop browser (keyboard/mouse optimized, tabular queues, side-by-side stock & order verification, PDF print/download).
- **Design Goal:** Single control tower for commercial verification, GST billing, dispatch, and collections.
- **Allowed Actions:**
  - Manage commercial & operational master data: Customers (incl. GSTIN & State), Suppliers, Products/SKUs, Price Lists, and Users/Role assignments (or User management restricted to Owner + Admin).
  - View **all** incoming orders from all salesmen, inspect stock availability (`On-Hand` vs `Reserved` vs `Ordered`).
  - Edit order quantities/prices (prior to invoice posting, strictly audit-logged), Request Correction, Approve, or Reject orders.
  - Generate GST Tax Invoices (`POSTED`), download GST Invoice PDF, trigger share link (WhatsApp/email intent).
  - Cancel posted invoices **strictly via Credit Note** (never hard-delete).
  - Record Dispatches (reducing finished-goods stock).
  - Record Customer Payments and allocate to open invoices.
  - Record Material Purchases (shared capability with Production Supervisor if factory desk handles supplier bills).
  - View all sales, inventory, purchase, and AR reports & export CSV/Excel/PDF.
- **Explicitly Forbidden Actions:**
  - Cannot delete a posted GST invoice, credit note, payment, or stock ledger row.
  - Cannot directly overwrite stock balances (all corrections must flow through source documents or logged `StockAdjustment` with mandatory reason).
  - Cannot alter BOM historical versions retroactively or edit completed production runs without creating an explicit logged audit trail / adjustment.
  - Cannot purge or modify Audit Logs.

### 1.3 Production Supervisor (`PRODUCTION_SUPERVISOR`)
- **Primary Device:** Tablet or desktop on the factory floor (large touch targets, clear unit selectors, quick batch multipliers, bright contrast).
- **Design Goal:** Capture purchases, BOM material issues, actual good output, damaged packaging, and wastage accurately on the floor.
- **Allowed Actions:**
  - View raw-material, packaging, and finished-goods stock balances and reorder alerts.
  - Record Raw Material & Packaging Purchases / Receipts from suppliers (per Section 3: *"Record raw-material receipts and issues"*).
  - Create / maintain active BOMs (or request/maintain recipes — see boundary note below with Admin).
  - Start Production Runs, issue raw materials against BOM, record actual good output, wastage, and damaged packaging, and record run-level labour/gas/power inputs.
  - View planned vs actual consumption, yield variances, and variance alerts for runs.
  - View Stock Summary, Stock Ledger, and Production Reports.
- **Explicitly Forbidden Actions:**
  - Cannot view customer sales orders (except aggregated production demand count if Stretch Goal "Production planning from pending orders" is enabled), customer receivables, or payment collections.
  - Cannot generate, approve, edit, or cancel Sales Orders, GST Invoices, Credit Notes, or Dispatches.
  - Cannot change product selling prices, customer GSTINs, or GST tax slabs.
  - Cannot overwrite stock balances directly outside receipts/runs/logged adjustments, and cannot delete completed production runs.

### 1.4 Accountant / Auditor (`ACCOUNTANT_AUDITOR`)
- **Primary Device:** Desktop browser (wide financial tables, filtering, reconciliation drill-downs, CSV/Excel/PDF exports).
- **Design Goal:** Complete, tamper-proof financial, tax, stock, and production audit visibility with **zero write access**.
- **Allowed Actions (Strictly Read-Only across operational & financial records):**
  - Read & export Sales Register (customer-wise, product-wise), Outward GST Summary (GSTR-1/3B format), Purchase Register, Stock Summary & full Stock Ledger (with source-document drill-down), Production Variance & Costing Reports, Payment/Collection Register, and Customer AR Ageing.
  - Read GST Invoice PDFs and Credit Note PDFs.
  - Read the complete immutable **Audit Trail Log** (who edited which invoice/run/price, when, and before/after values).
- **Explicitly Forbidden Actions:**
  - **All write/mutate/delete actions are strictly forbidden:** cannot create/edit/approve/reject orders, cannot raise or cancel invoices, cannot record payments, cannot record purchases or production runs, cannot modify master data (customers, BOMs, prices, tax rates), and cannot create stock adjustments.

### 1.5 Owner (`OWNER`)
- **Primary Device:** Responsive dashboard (mobile phone when travelling + desktop at office).
- **Design Goal:** Real-time visibility into factory health, anomalies, cash dues, and unit margins without needing to dig through ledgers.
- **Allowed Actions:**
  - View executive dashboard: Today's orders & revenue, current raw & finished stock levels, variance alerts exceeding tolerance (`> 3%`), approximate cost per packet & selling margin per SKU, and total / overdue customer receivables.
  - Drill down into all operational reports, GST summaries, costing breakdowns, and Audit Logs (read-only across operational transactions by default, with optional administrative governance actions like setting variance tolerance % or managing user accounts).
- **Explicitly Forbidden Actions:**
  - Should **not** silently overwrite stock balances, edit posted GST invoices, or bypass approval/audit trails. (Even if Owner holds super-admin user-management rights, financial/ledger immutability rules apply equally to Owner.)

## 2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Permission Matrix

Legend: **C** = Create | **R** = Read | **U** = Update (Draft/Logged) | **A** = Approve/Post/Complete | **X** = Forbidden | **Own** = Scoped to user's own records

| Domain / Capability | Salesman | Factory Admin | Production Supervisor | Accountant / Auditor | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Auth & User Management** | Own profile | C/R/U (Ops users) | Own profile | Own profile | C/R/U (All users) |
| **Customers & Suppliers Master** | R (Customers) | C/R/U | R (Suppliers) / C* | R | R |
| **Finished Products & Price List** | R | C/R/U | R (Specs only) | R | R/U (Prices) |
| **Raw/Pkg Materials & BOM Master** | X | R/U | C/R/U | R | R |
| **Sales Orders (Mobile / Queue)** | C/R/U (Own draft/corr) | R/U/A (Approve/Reject) | X (or demand qty only) | R | R |
| **GST Invoices & Credit Notes** | R (Own PDF) | C/A (Post & CN Cancel) | X | R | R |
| **Dispatches (FG Deduction)** | R (Status) | C/A | X | R | R |
| **Customer Payments & Allocations** | X | C/R | X | R | R |
| **Material Purchases / Receipts** | X | C/R/A | C/R/A | R | R |
| **Production Runs & Issue/Output** | X | R | C/U/A (Complete) | R | R |
| **Run Overheads & Costing Inputs** | X | R/U | C/R/U | R | R/U |
| **Stock Adjustments (Logged)** | X | C/A (Reason req.) | C (Draft) / X* | R | R |
| **Stock Ledger & Balances** | X (Non-blocking FG badge only) | R | R | R | R |
| **Audit Trail Logs** | X | R | X | R | R |
| **Monthly Auditor Exports (CSV/PDF)** | Own sales summary | R / Export | Production/Stock R | R / Export All | R / Export All |
| **Hard-Delete Posted Docs / Ledger** | **X** | **X** | **X** | **X** | **X** |

> *Notes on Permission Boundaries:*
> - `C*` / `X*`: Whether Production Supervisor can directly post Stock Adjustments (e.g., damaged stock found on floor) vs requiring Admin approval is a configurable segregation-of-duties control (recommended: Supervisor requests adjustment, Admin or Owner approves; both logged).
> - **Hard-Deletion Ban:** **No role** — including Owner and Factory Admin — can hard-delete a posted purchase, completed production run, posted GST invoice, credit note, payment receipt, stock ledger entry, or audit log entry. Corrections use reversals/credit notes/logged adjustments.

## 3. Data Visibility & API Security Rules

1. **Server-Side Enforcement Only:** UI hiding is never security; every backend API endpoint / service action validates role + record ownership (e.g., `Salesman A` requesting `Salesman B`'s order ID returns `403 Forbidden`).
2. **Cost Confidentiality:** Field Salesman responses **must strip** raw material rates, BOM formulas, production run costs, and gross margins so trade secrets and factory margins are never leaked to field phones.
3. **Read-Only Guarantee for Auditor:** Any non-`GET` mutation call with `ACCOUNTANT_AUDITOR` token is rejected at middleware level (`403 Forbidden`).
